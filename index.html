Sources and more numbers

The pound has lost 98% of its value since 2019. Banks became insolvent, and more than a third of people were pushed into poverty. World Bank, opens in a new tab

IARC GLOBOCAN 2024: 42,382 people living with a cancer found in the last five years. 8,706 cancer deaths. About 1 in 4 people develops cancer before age 75 (24.2%). Breast cancer caused 978 deaths, second among cancers. 9,615 people are living with breast cancer found in the last five years. 38.7% is 3,404 of 8,798 new cancers among women. IARC fact sheet (PDF), opens in a new tab

New cases rose 162% from 1990 to 2023, the fastest rise of any country (Global Burden of Disease 2023, IHME, The Lancet, 2025). Deaths rose 80%. Cases have been rising about 4% a year. In 2023 there were 233.5 new cases per 100,000 people. GBD 2023 / IHME, opens in a new tab

At Hôtel-Dieu de France — a different Beirut hospital, not AUBMC — 32% interrupted treatment, mostly chemotherapy (Eid et al., 2024, n=182). In February 2022, 43% of those households earned under $300 a month. Even with coverage, 70% paid for medication themselves. AUBMC’s own costs are not on this page. PMC11145953, opens in a new tab

The Naef K. Basile Cancer Institute at AUBMC enrolls about 2,500 new cancer patients a year. AUBMC, opens in a new tab

This fund, as AUBMC reported it: 12 patients (9 women, 3 men), all Lebanese. 19 encounters — 16 for inpatient treatment, 3 for outpatient imaging. In July 2026 it helped three Lebanese women in treatment for breast cancer. Diagnoses for the other nine were not supplied. Social services found each patient highly eligible. Most had been displaced by the war.

AUB publishes a 4% spending policy, on the 20-quarter moving average. AUB’s example, not this fund’s size: a $3 million endowment pays about $120,000 in year one. AUB Boldly FAQ, opens in a new tab This page does not say what share of a gift reaches a patient.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

AUB is a 501(c)(3) organization registered in the US. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by US law. This is not tax advice; consult your own advisor.

Non-US donors: this page makes no deductibility claim outside the US.

Who decides which patients get help?

AUBMC social services and the medical team. Not the founder, and not the donor.

How do I know it reaches patients?

The fund reports to donors. AUB files a Form 990. ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, opens in a new tab
