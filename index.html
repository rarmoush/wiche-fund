Why cost matters

In a 2024–25 study of cancer patients in Lebanon, more than half (52.5%) waited over two weeks to start treatment, with cost among the main reasons. Patients without insurance were more likely to receive incomplete treatment.

In the same study (244 patients, 5 Beirut hospitals, not a national figure), 57% had no medical insurance.

  4. 4

    Care at AUBMC

    Help is for AUBMC (AUH) patients. The hospital screens each patient and thoroughly reviews them in its patient financial assessment before help is approved.

Help is given without discrimination: any eligible AUBMC patient, whatever their religion, sect, nationality, background, or gender.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

AUB is a 501(c)(3) organization registered in the US. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by US law. This is not tax advice; consult your own advisor.

Non-US donors: this page makes no deductibility claim outside the US.

Who decides which patients get help?

AUBMC’s patient financial assessment. Each patient is screened and thoroughly reviewed before help is approved. Not the founder, and not the donor.
